BANNERLORDAI — STRATEGIC COMMITMENT v0.20O OFFLINE REVIEW
Date: 2026-09-18 Pacific.

Purpose:
Introduce the smallest data-driven commitment/hysteresis mechanism after v0.20N proved real final-stack objective churn.

Evidence basis:
v0.20N mature-world sample:
- 501 decisions / 106 actors
- 45 parsed objective switches
- 42 switches retained a still-legal previous objective in the candidate list
- same-state eligible switches: 23
- cross-state eligible switches: 19
- same-state <=5%: 1
- same-state <=10%: 3
- same-state <=20%: 3
- median objective age ~6.06 campaign hours

Offline counterfactual:
Same-state-only factor sensitivity:
- x1.02–x1.05 would retain 1 switch
- x1.075 would retain 2
- x1.10 would retain 3
- x1.125/x1.15 still only 3 in this sample

First intervention is intentionally narrower than x1.10 across all same-state switches:
- previous objective must still be present in current candidate list
- natural winner must differ from previous objective
- previous coarse state must equal natural winner coarse state
- objective age must be <=12 campaign hours
- previous objective receives at most x1.10
- no intervention when previous objective vanished
- no intervention across coarse-state changes
- no movement/pathfinding/action invention
- factor enters only through StrategicDecisionComposer

Why 12 hours:
The observed strategic reconsideration rhythm is often ~6 campaign hours. Twelve hours bounds commitment to roughly two common reconsideration cycles. It is an initial engineering window, not a universal behavioral truth.
This excludes Ecarand's ~12.12h Recover->Recover near-switch while retaining the much clearer Yngvar (~6.06h / 0.3% gap) and Vasilia (~2.02h / 5.05% gap) opportunities in the current sample.

Architecture:
- ActorStrategicBlackboard remains the per-actor state owner.
- StrategicCommitmentLayer reads the previous objective/state from blackboard.
- StrategicCommitmentLayer runs immediately before StrategicDecisionComposer.Complete.
- Observe mode calculates exact would-retain counterfactual and writes nothing.
- Apply mode only contributes x1.10 when it would actually retain the previous legal objective.
- composer remains the ONLY SetBehaviorScore owner.
