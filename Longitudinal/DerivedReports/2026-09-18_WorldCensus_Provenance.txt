BANNERLORDAI — WORLD CENSUS PROVENANCE
Date: 2026-09-18 Pacific

User-confirmed origin:
The first world-census effort began as an economic research tool for Vadrios's kingdom. Its original purpose was to identify production, market conditions, trading traffic, routes, food movement, and money flow so trade routes and passive income could be improved.

Evolution:
1. Economic/trade route mapping for Vadrios.
2. Settlement, village, market, supply and traffic census.
3. Army/lord-party/world-state monitoring.
4. Longitudinal world model and analyzer reports.
5. Objective evidence layer for AI reasoning, causal validation, and future dynasty comparisons.

Provenance rule:
The Y1084 Master Recorder history is instrumentation/lab evidence and must not be silently merged into Calastides dynasty chronology. It remains valuable for systems research and validating economy/traffic/supply observation.

Dynasty-specific evidence remains separate:
- vadrios_native_history.json: 1,928 retained native events through Spring 5, 1135.
- vadrios_pause_economy/supply/traffic/market/military/lordparties: frozen Spring 1135 Calastides-world evidence.

Future use:
Pair date-scoped character knowledge/personality with objective censuses available at that date. This allows comparison of what Nethor/Manan/Vadrios believed or perceived against what the larger world was objectively doing, without giving the character omniscience.
