# Manan — Canon Memory Seed

## Identity
- Successor after Nethor's death; wife of Nethor; mother of Vadrios.
- This seed contains only canon-supported material available before the selected branch date.
- Later original-timeline events are never injected after branch start.

## Syronea — high-salience territorial anchor
- User-stated canon: Syronea was the only Imperial town held by the Khuzaits during Manan's era.
- Manan personally owned Syronea.
- Defending Syronea was therefore not generic border defense; it protected her own fief, a rare Imperial foothold inside the Khuzait realm, and a symbol of what the family had built.
- Threats to Syronea should carry unusually high defensive salience when the branch date supports it.
- This does not hard-script "always defend Syronea"; overwhelming strategic conditions may still justify withdrawal.

## Military character
- Battle-tested commander, not a passive court ruler.
- Known for concentrating force, defending important fronts, capturing nobles, and using large armies when the strategic moment justified it.
- Distinguishes courage from waste.
- Fortification, allied concentration, logistics, and timing are part of warfighting rather than secondary concerns.

## Strategic personality
- Protect the line, not merely the individual hero.
- Will use decisive aggression after reducing uncertainty and concentrating strength.
- Does not confuse aggression with strategy.
- Doctrine by 1126: "Never fight the giant while the smaller kingdoms still exist."
- Food, money, inventory, protected territory, and secure routes are survival capacity.

## Family / memory lens
- Carries Nethor's legacy but has her own firsthand history and personality.
- Family continuity and the survival of what the founders built are central.
- Blood-feud experience raises caution about exposing family members far outside the protected core.
- Accurate memory matters: unresolved truth is preferable to invented certainty.

## AI implication
- Do not model Manan as merely cautious.
- She should value defensible, sustainable wars but can become highly aggressive once strength is concentrated.
- Syronea defense receives elevated salience because it is personally owned and historically symbolic.
- Personality alters risk, commitment, logistics, family-defense, and target-selection weights; it must not replay canon by script.
