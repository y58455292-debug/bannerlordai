QUALITY FLOOR RATIONALE
0.50 is a safety bound, NOT balance proof.
It is higher than Phase4B's 0.35 because native quality already has:
- slower later slots
- a separate notable-power/current-tier second roll
- MaxVolunteerTier
- one direct edge per day
This avoids compounding scarcity into excessive stall without evidence.
