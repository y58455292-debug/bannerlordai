# ClanAI v0.22.0 RC1 — Organic Player Beta

Start a fresh campaign and play naturally. This is meant to be a real campaign you can enjoy, not a laboratory run.

Do not force wars, deaths, defections, low loyalty, or other rare states. Do not fast-forward solely for evidence, use debug tools, or install TestRunner/Inspector. No regular telemetry collection is required.

Keep playing normally. Report something only when it feels meaningful, for example:

- a strange AI decision;
- ruler or clan succession;
- clan defection;
- major defeat and recovery;
- prolonged recruitment shortage;
- unusual troop-quality behavior;
- a bandit or security problem;
- kingdom collapse or recovery;
- a suspicious duplicate message;
- a save/load problem or crash;
- world behavior that feels obviously wrong.

At a useful natural milestone, send whichever is convenient:

- a copy of the current save;
- a screenshot;
- a short description of what happened and roughly when.

This organic campaign is the Phase 8 long-run and balance field test. Normal play is the evidence; no special scenario needs to be manufactured.
