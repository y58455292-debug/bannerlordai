# Contributing as External Research Liaison

Research quality is measured by uncertainty reduced, not words produced.

Before opening a PR:
- use the packet template;
- cite sources precisely;
- distinguish evidence from inference;
- record contradictory evidence;
- estimate performance/scale implications;
- identify license constraints for reusable code;
- define the smallest future BannerlordAI test;
- keep mutation authority unchanged unless Front Office explicitly opens a separate gate.

Front Office may ask for revisions when:
- evidence quality is weak;
- conclusions exceed sources;
- performance impact is ignored;
- local Bannerlord constraints are not considered;
- a proposal duplicates an accepted subsystem;
- the packet lacks falsification/negative controls.

Do not commit secrets, game binaries, protected saves, or unlicensed copyrighted assets.
